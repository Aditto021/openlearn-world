Model, texture, render.

Blender is a complete, professional-grade 3D creation suite — and it costs nothing. Modeling, sculpting, animation, simulation, video editing, and full Python scripting, all in one free app.

Interesting facts

What makes Blender different.

🆓

Completely free

No subscription, no watermark, no feature paywall — released under the GPL open-source license since 1994, maintained by the nonprofit Blender Foundation.

🎬

Full production pipeline

Modeling, sculpting, rigging, animation, cloth/fluid/smoke simulation, VFX compositing, and video editing — in one application.

🐍

Scriptable with Python

Nearly everything in Blender can be automated or extended with Python, powering a huge ecosystem of free and paid add-ons.

☀️

Two render engines

Eevee renders in real time as you work; Cycles is a physically-based path tracer for photorealistic final renders.

🎥

Made real films

The Blender Foundation’s own studio has released open movies like Sprite Fox and Charge — free to watch, with source files included.

⌨️

Keyboard-driven

G (grab), R (rotate), S (scale), Tab (edit mode) — a handful of shortcuts cover most of daily modeling work.
